About Jamat Raza-E-Mustafa (JRM) Antophill

History, Mission & Leadership

Historical Origin & Bareilly Sharif Heritage

Jamat Raza-E-Mustafa is an international Sunni Islamic organization founded in 1920 in Bareilly Sharif, Uttar Pradesh, by the eminent reviver of Islam, Ala Hazrat Imam Ahmed Raza Khan Fazil-e-Bareilvi (رضي الله عنه), and consolidated under the spiritual leadership of Huzoor Mufti-e-Azam Hind Maulana Mustafa Raza Khan Qadri.

The organization was established to preserve traditional Islamic theology, uphold the creed of the Ahle Sunnah wal Jama'ah, educate the community through systematic deeni learning, and offer social welfare in adherence to Islamic Shariah.

Antophill Wadala Chapter

The Antophill, Wadala branch operates as an active regional chapter in Mumbai, headquartered at Akbar Suleman Compound (Bada Maidan), Antophill, Wadala East, Mumbai 400037.

Under the supreme spiritual patronage of Huzoor Qaid-e-Millat Madda Zillahul Aali and supervised locally by accredited Ulama-e-Ahle Sunnat, the Antophill chapter conducts its signature monthly gathering—the Mahana Sunni Deeni Nashisht—along with Milad conferences, Urs commemorations, and community assistance.

Our Leadership & Administration

The local operations and community coordination are managed by Zain and the dedicated JRM Antophill Committee Zimmedaran, working hand-in-hand with neighborhood elders and contributors (Khuddam).
